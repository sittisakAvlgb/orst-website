# Help-panel mascot

The contact shortcut in the bottom corner opens a help panel. Its green greeting band shows a mascot on the right
(`[[assistant-mascot]]` in `src/templates/partials/assistant.html`). Until a file is added here the band shows the
greeting on its own.

| Use | File name (any one extension) |
| --- | --- |
| Mascot in the help panel | `mascot.png` |

- **Transparent background (`.png`), square, about 400 × 400 px, under 120 KB.**
- The figure should stand at the bottom right of the image, facing left towards the greeting; it is shown at about
  136 × 136 px on the dark green band, so keep the shapes bold and use the emblem's gold and green.
